Alternate Scenario • Tenant Verification Negative Case • Rejection & Resubmission

What If Your Organization Request is Rejected?

If legal entity information cannot be verified, SparkX superadmins will decline the request and provide specific feedback for resubmission.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Rejection Guide
  • 1Why Rejection Occurs: Requests are rejected if the legal entity name does not match official tax records, the registration number (NPWP) is invalid, or details are insufficient to verify the organization.
  • 2Email Rejection Notice: You will receive an email titled “About your request for [Company Name]” with the explanation: “We reviewed your request to set up [Company Name] on SparkX and couldn't approve it. The details weren't enough for us to verify the organization.”
  • 3Web Portal Rejection Banner: Logging in to the SparkX web app displays an alert banner explaining the decision and inviting a new request.
  • 4How to Resubmit: Click Open SparkX (or log in), review the feedback, click Create a new organization, and re-submit your registration with complete, accurate legal documents and verified corporate credentials.
Need assistance with verification? Contact the SparkX support team with your corporate registration documents for priority verification.

Employee Onboarding • Access Requirements

Welcome to SparkX Employee Self-Service (ESS)

Your official step-by-step guideline to activating your personal employee account, setting security credentials, and exploring the ESS platform.

Modern Digital Workplace for Employees

SparkX Employee Self-Service (ESS) empowers you to perform daily attendance clock-in, request leaves, track reimbursement claims, and manage your employment profile with complete transparency and zero paperwork.

Strict Access Policy • Invitation Only

Important: Access is Strictly by HR Invitation Only

Unlike HR Administrators or Company Founders who can register a new organization, regular employees cannot register directly on the SparkX website. If you attempt to sign up directly via the public registration screen, the system will assume you are creating a new company and will prompt you for corporate legal details, permanent operating currency, and corporate tax documents (NPWP). Regular employees cannot join an existing employer’s workspace through public sign-up.

To access your company’s Employee Self-Service (ESS) workspace, your company HR Administrator must first register your profile in the corporate employee directory and dispatch an official email invitation. Each invitation contains a secure, cryptographically signed one-time activation link that is strictly valid for 7 calendar days and bound directly to your designated email address.

✕
No Public Sign-Up Direct registration is only for setting up new legal entities.
✉
Issued by HR Admin Your corporate email must be registered in the company directory.
⏱
7-Day Expiration Tokens expire after 7 days and must be re-sent if unused.
Haven't received an invitation email yet? Check with your company HR Admin or manager to ensure your work email address was added to the employee directory.

Official HR Invitation Email

An active invitation email sent to your corporate or personal address with a 7-day validity token.

Strong Master Password

Prepare a secure password of at least 8 characters (or prepare your company Google account for SSO).

Browser or Smartphone

SparkX is fully responsive across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop workstations.
